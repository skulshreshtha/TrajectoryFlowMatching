The dynamics of the Neural SDE with a non-Markovian diffusion term are given by:

\[
dx_t = f_\theta(x_t, t) \, dt + \xi_t \, dt,
\]

where \( \xi_t \) evolves according to:

\[
d\xi_t = -\lambda \xi_t \, dt + \sigma \, dW_t.
\]

Here:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \( f_\theta(x_t, t) \) is the neural network parameterized drift term,
    \item \( \xi_t \) represents the non-Markovian diffusion term,
    \item \( \lambda > 0 \) is the rate of mean reversion,
    \item \( \sigma \) is the diffusion intensity,
    \item \( W_t \) is a standard Wiener process.
\end{itemize}
